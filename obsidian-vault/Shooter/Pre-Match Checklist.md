# Pre-match checklist (every match)

Back to [[FTC Home]] · Why: [[Shooter Fix List - Night Before Competition]]

- [ ] Freshly charged battery every match; check voltage before queueing (telemetry shows it now).
- [ ] In the pit, spin the shooter 5 s; confirm telemetry reaches target and **"At speed" turns true**.
- [ ] After any power cycle, confirm the PIDF on telemetry matches the hardcoded values in `ShooterConfig`.
- [ ] Check motor bracket bolts, hub paint line, and hood gap mark; look for cracks in the prints.
- [ ] Spin up while driving to the shooting spot, then **stop** and shoot. Drive motors pull down the voltage the flywheel needs to recover.
- [ ] Shoot only from the taped spots that match the presets.
- [ ] Never hold more than **4** balls (G407). A 5-ball load is fine in practice, illegal in a match.
- [ ] After every TIP the up CELL swaps ends: use the taped spot for that CELL, and wait for the HIVE to settle before shooting. See [[BIOBUZZ Rules and Scoring]].
- [ ] Agree with the partner: both LEAVE in AUTO and both PARK at the end, for the SWARM RP (16 pts). Tell them which way our AUTO LEAVEs, who shoots when, and keep out of each other's lane at the HIVE.
- [ ] In the match: shoot only while stopped, and head back to PARK with about 10 s left (5 pts).
- [ ] After the match: swap the battery, and write one line in [[Competition Day Tips]] (shots taken, shots in, what broke).

Match balls come from the field, so you can't pick the good ones. The shooter has to cope with ball-to-ball differences, which is one more reason the squeeze must stay steady.
