# Small fixes without reprinting (stiffen the shooter)

Back to [[FTC Home]] · Why these matter: [[Shooter Fix List - Night Before Competition]] · Evidence: [[Shooter Video Evidence]]

No time to reprint big parts, so use hardware from the kit box. In the video, the motor moved 1.5–3 mm on every shot. That changes the hood squeeze from ball to ball, so these fixes go **before** any PIDF tuning.

## Do these first (about 15 min each)
- [ ] **Flywheel set screw:** tighten it (blue threadlocker on metal-to-metal only), then draw a paint line across the hub and shaft. If the line splits, the wheel slipped.
- [ ] **Brace the motor to the chassis with metal:** a goBILDA/REV bracket, a short piece of U-channel or extrusion, or standoffs from the motor face to the frame. Even one brace on the side away from the hood helps a lot.
- [ ] **Make a triangle:** a diagonal strap or standoff from the top of the shooter down to the frame stops it leaning side to side.
- [ ] **Washers under screw heads on plastic:** spread the load so screws don't sink into the print. Use **nylock nuts** so vibration can't loosen them.
- [ ] **Lock the hood gap:** set the squeeze with washers as spacers, or a thin strip of foam or grip tape on the hood. Mark its position with paint. Squeeze is about 15–25% of the ball's diameter: firm, but the ball still rolls through by hand.
- [ ] **Smooth the hood:** PTFE or electrical tape over printed layer lines where the ball slides.
- [ ] **Ball guide lip:** a strip of polycarbonate, sheet aluminium or stiff plastic, held with screws or VHB tape, so every ball meets the wheel in the same spot.
- [ ] Tie the loose wires away from the flywheel.

## Better than zip ties
| Instead of | Use | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zip ties holding a part | Metal bracket + bolts + nylock | Doesn't stretch or slip |
| Screws straight into plastic | Bolt through with a nut, or a heat-set insert (soldering iron, 2 min each) | Threads in plastic strip out |
| Gluing a cracked print | Metal plate bolted across the crack | Glue fails under vibration |
| Plastic spacer | Aluminium standoff | Doesn't squash |
| Zip tie around the motor can | Hose clamp or a goBILDA motor clamp mount | Clamps tight and doesn't stretch |

**Don't** put threadlocker on printed parts: it can crack some plastics.

## Pass test
Push the flywheel toward the hood by hand. If you see anything move, add another brace. Then shoot 5 balls and check that the paint marks haven't moved.

## Spares to bring
Spare printed bracket and hood, nylock nuts, washers, standoffs, a hex key set, tape, paint pen, VHB tape.
